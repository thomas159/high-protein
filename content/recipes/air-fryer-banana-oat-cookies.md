faq:
- question: Can I use instant porridge oats instead of rolled oats?
  answer: Rolled oats deliver the best chewy cookie texture. Quick oats can be used if that is all you have, but the cookies will be softer and slightly more cake-like.
- question: Can I make this in a standard oven?
  answer: Yes. Bake on a lined tray in a preheated oven at 190°C for 10-12 minutes until the edges turn golden and firm.
- question: How do I scale this recipe for the whole family?
  answer: Simply double or triple the quantities using whole bananas. Arrange the shaped cookies on parchment paper in your air fryer basket without overlapping so the air circulates freely.
steps:
- Start by peeling half a ripe, spotty banana and mashing it thoroughly in a small bowl with a fork until it forms a smooth, lump-free liquid purée.
- Add the gluten-free rolled oats and ground cinnamon to the bowl, folding together gently until a thick, textured dough forms that easily holds its shape.
- Divide and shape the mixture into three small, round cookie discs, then arrange them on a piece of parchment paper cut to fit your air fryer basket.
- Firmly press two dark chocolate chips into the top of each cookie so they nestle securely into the oats and banana base.
- Air fry at 180°C for 8 minutes, until the edges turn golden and lightly crisp while the centre remains soft and chewy.
- Break one cookie in half while warm to reveal the jammy banana interior, and enjoy immediately while the chocolate is deliciously melted.
