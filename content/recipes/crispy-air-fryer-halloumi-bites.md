tipsTitle: Common Mistakes To Avoid
tips:
- 'Slicing Too Thin: If you cut them too small, they soften too quickly and can fall through the basket. Keep them in sturdy, bite-sized cubes of around 2 cm for the best squeaky bite.'
- 'Adding Salt Early: Halloumi is cured in brine and already naturally salty. Always taste before adding salt—you only need a tiny pinch of flaky sea salt at the very end for texture.'
- 'Crowding the Basket: Give each cube breathing room. Touching blocks will trap steam rather than blister into that iconic deep-golden crust.'

faq:
- question: Do I need any oil to air fry halloumi?
  answer: Not at all. Halloumi has plenty of natural fat that crisps the exterior as it heats. Skipping added oil keeps the snack light and saves unnecessary calories.
- question: Why did my halloumi cubes melt or stick to the basket?
  answer: This happens when cubes are cut too small or thin, or if the air fryer was not hot enough. Cut sturdy cubes of around 2 cm and keep them spaced apart so the hot air circulates freely.
- question: Can I scale this recipe for more servings?
  answer: Absolutely. Just increase the ingredients proportionally and keep the cubes in a single layer without overcrowding the basket so they blister evenly.
steps:
- Cut the halloumi cheese into even, sturdy bite-sized cubes of around 2 cm. Do not add any oil, as the cheese contains plenty of natural fats to fry itself.
- Place the cubes into the air fryer basket in a single layer, ensuring they are not touching so the edges can form that iconic blistered crust.
- Air fry at 200°C for 8 minutes, checking around the 6-minute mark until the cubes turn a vivid, deep golden-amber.
- Transfer the sizzling halloumi bites to a small serving plate and immediately drizzle the runny honey in thin ribbons across the hot surface.
- Shower the bites with red chilli flakes and picked fresh thyme leaves for a high-contrast sweet, spicy, and herbal punch.
- Finish with a delicate pinch of flaky sea salt and serve immediately while the center is warm and squeaky.
