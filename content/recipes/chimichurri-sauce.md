faq:
- question: Why is my chimichurri bitter?
  answer: You probably used a food processor. Blitzing olive oil at high speed turns it bitter and bruises the parsley. Chop by hand and stir the oil in gently.
- question: Can I make this ahead of time?
  answer: Yes. Making it a day early lets the flavours marry perfectly in the fridge.
steps:
- Finely chop the parsley, garlic, and chilli by hand with a sharp knife. You want the herbs fine but still leafy and bright green, not a bruised, dark paste.
- Whisk the vinegar, salt, and garlic together in a small bowl. Leave it for two minutes so the vinegar softens the raw, burning edge of the garlic.
- Stir in the parsley, chilli, oregano, and black pepper until every bit of green is coated and glossy with vinegar.
- Pour in the olive oil and stir gently with a spoon until just combined. It should look loose and speckled green, with the oil pooling slightly around the herbs.
- Taste and add a pinch more salt or a splash more vinegar if it needs more zing. Serve straight away, or leave it for fifteen minutes so the herbs release their full flavour.
