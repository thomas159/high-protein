faq:
- question: Can I use frozen berries instead of fresh?
  answer: Yes, but thaw them thoroughly on kitchen paper first to soak up excess liquid, otherwise the cottage cheese base turns watery.
- question: How can I make cottage cheese less lumpy?
  answer: Blitz it in a food processor or with a stick blender for 30 seconds. It whips into a silky smooth yoghurt-like consistency that tastes fantastic.
- question: Can I swap the honey for maple syrup?
  answer: Absolutely. Pure maple syrup or a zero-calorie sugar-free pancake syrup work brilliantly to keep the carbs even lower.
steps:
- Spoon the cottage cheese into the centre of a wide, shallow bowl and spread it out gently with the back of a spoon to create an even, creamy base.
- Scatter the fresh blueberries, raspberries, and blackberries across the surface, distributing them evenly so every spoonful gets a balance of fruit and cheese.
- Sprinkle the flaked almonds (slivered almonds) over the berries to add a crisp, nutty crunch that contrasts with the soft dairy.
- Scatter the chia seeds across the top, allowing them to settle on the surface and absorb any excess fruit juices.
- Finish with a light drizzle of honey across the fruit and nuts, followed by a pinch of cinnamon or fresh lemon zest if you fancy extra depth.
