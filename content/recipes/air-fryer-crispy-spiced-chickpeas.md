faq:
- question: Why didn't my chickpeas turn crispy in the air fryer?
  answer: Chickpeas won't crisp if they enter the air fryer damp. Water creates steam, resulting in a soft, chewy texture. Dry them thoroughly between sheets of kitchen paper (paper towels) until completely matte before seasoning.
- question: Is removing the skins mandatory?
  answer: Not mandatory, but highly recommended if you want maximum crunch. The thin translucent skins trap moisture, so gently rubbing them off between sheets of kitchen paper (paper towels) helps achieve that signature shatter crunch.
- question: How do I know when the chickpeas are fully cooked?
  answer: Shake the basket around the 15-minute mark. When they are ready, they will sound like little pebbles rattling against the metal basket, looking uniformly golden and feeling hard to the touch.
steps:
- Drain the canned chickpeas thoroughly in a colander. Transfer them between two layers of kitchen paper (paper towels) and dry them completely until the skins look dull, matte, and bone-dry.
- Toss the dry chickpeas into a small bowl. Mist lightly with olive oil spray, then sprinkle over the smoked paprika, ground cumin, and garlic salt, tossing until evenly coated.
- Pour the seasoned chickpeas into the air fryer basket and spread them out into a single layer so every chickpea has its own personal space to roast.
- Air fry at 200°C for 15 minutes, giving the basket a good shake every 5 minutes to ensure an even, all-over golden crunch.
- Check for doneness around the 15-minute mark: they are ready when they sound like hard little pebbles hitting the side of the basket—firm, golden, and completely crispy.
- Transfer to a small bowl and scatter with extra flaky sea salt and a delicate dust of smoked paprika while still hot for maximum flavour.
