tipsTitle: Common Mistakes To Avoid
tips:
- 'Dry Them Bone-Dry: If your courgettes (zucchini) are damp, the parmesan will slide right off and turn into a mushy paste. Pat the cut batons thoroughly between sheets of kitchen paper (paper towels) until completely matte before coating.'
- 'Never Pre-Salt: Do not salt the courgettes before cooking. Salt draws out internal moisture like a sponge and will make your fries soggy. Save any extra salt for the very end.'
- 'Give Them Personal Space: Spread the fries out in the air fryer basket in a single layer with room between each baton. Overcrowding traps steam instead of letting circulating air blast the parmesan into a crisp shell.'

faq:
- question: Why did my courgette fries turn out soggy?
  answer: Courgettes (zucchini) have very high water content. If they were not patted bone-dry with sheets of kitchen paper (paper towels) or if salt was added before cooking, water escapes and turns the cheese coating soggy. Keep them dry and avoid overcrowding the basket.
- question: Do they really taste like mozzarella sticks?
  answer: Yes! The blistered parmesan forms a salty, savoury outer shell that closely mimics the crust and flavour of a mozzarella stick without flour, eggs, or deep frying.
- question: Can I make these in a standard oven?
  answer: Yes. Bake on a baking-parchment-lined wire rack at 220°C for 15-18 minutes until the cheese is blistered and golden. An air fryer will still yield the crispiest result due to rapid air circulation.
steps:
- Slice your courgettes (zucchini) into uniform batons, aiming for about 7-8 cm long so they have a sturdy mozzarella-stick feel. Pat them thoroughly bone-dry between sheets of kitchen paper (paper towels) until completely matte.
- In a small bowl, mix your parmesan, Italian herbs, and garlic powder until the seasoning forms a fine, savoury dust.
- Toss the courgette batons in the parmesan mixture, pressing the cheese firmly into the surface so it forms a textured, even crust.
- Arrange them in the air fryer basket in a single layer with space between each baton for the air to circulate freely.
- Air fry at 200°C for 10 minutes until the parmesan crust is deeply golden, sizzling, and blistered.
- Plate immediately with your garlic Greek yoghurt dip on the side and a light scatter of red chilli flakes for a kick of heat.
